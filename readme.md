## Open-Source Masters in CS @ AIT

### Author's Note
My ultimate goal here is to share most of the syllabus and homework problems
that i have worked on and collected throughout all the coursework that i have
done during my Master's in CS @ AIT.

### Legal Note
I will not share the lecture notes or slides provided by the teaching / academic
staff of AIT. All the content shared here are my own study notes and homework problems
i had worked on.

### How to navigate

```
osmcs/
├── y1s1/                          Year 1 — Semester 1
│   ├── CN/                        Computer Networks
│   ├── DMM/                       Database Modeling & Management
│   ├── FSAD/                      Full Stack Application Development
│   └── TOC/                       Theory of Computation
└── y1s2/                          Year 1 — Semester 2
    ├── ALGO/                      Algorithm Design & Analysis
    ├── ML/                        Machine Learning
    ├── MLOps/                     Machine Learning Operations
    └── PLC/                       Programming Languages & Compilers
```

Each course folder contains:
- `README.md` — course overview, topics covered, and how materials are organised
- `notes/` — my own study notes written in Markdown
- `projects/` or `labs/` or `assignments/` — my own homework and project work
